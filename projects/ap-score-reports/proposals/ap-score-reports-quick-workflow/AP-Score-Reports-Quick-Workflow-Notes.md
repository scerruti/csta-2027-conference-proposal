# AP Scores to Instructional Action Collaborator Notes

## Working direction

This document captures collaborative notes for the concise AP score-report workflow version. It should preserve the same core message while making the pacing and scope more compact.

## Core ideas to keep

- Teachers need a practical way to turn AP score data into instructional decisions.
- Aggregate averages often obscure important patterns in student performance.
- Score-band analysis can make next steps more visible and actionable.
- Diagnostic questions can reveal instructional gaps beyond what broad report categories show.
- The content should feel immediate and useful for classroom planning and AP Classroom work.

## Feedback themes

### Strengths of the quick-workflow version

- The framing is clear and accessible for a fast conference format.
- The workflow is easy to explain in a short time.
- The tool and worksheet give the session a concrete resource participants can use later.

### Areas to simplify

- Keep the structure tight and avoid too much content in a 20-minute format.
- Focus on a few high-leverage ideas rather than multiple detailed examples.
- Remove or compress anything that is more appropriate to the fuller AP score-report analysis proposal.
- Keep the pacing around a single narrative arc: data challenge, diagnosis, action.

## Open questions

- Is the quick-workflow version better framed as a quick overview or a snapshot of the broader process?
- Should it emphasize the tool more strongly, or should it focus on the instructional strategy?
- Is the audience likely to want concrete examples or a quick conceptual model?

## Possible revision ideas

- Make the title and abstract even more direct and teacher-facing.
- Distill the session outline to four key moments instead of five or six.
- Keep the practical takeaways limited to the highest-value actions.
- Use one clear example to demonstrate the workflow without overloading the quick-workflow version.

## Final note

The quick-workflow version should feel like a compact teaser for the broader AP score-report analysis proposal: enough to spark interest, but brief enough to fit a compact presentation format.
