# AP Scores to Instructional Action: A Quick Workflow

## Purpose

The 20-minute mini-session. It is the "why": it gives an overview of the [process](../../README.md#the-process), provides the resources, and explains the motivation, without working through the process in detail. It is more theory and less hands-on than the breakout.

## Relationship to the Breakout

The breakout is the hands-on "how" for the same process. Each session stands alone, and the two may compete for acceptance.

## Agreed Design

**Opening:** ask "Are you data-driven?" Most attendees will say yes. Then define it: the data identifies where to look, the teacher's experience forms a hypothesis, and new data tests it. By that definition, most attendees are data-aware, which sets up the rest of the session.

**Discussion built in:** reviewers marked the mini down for being lecture-heavy, so every stage includes discussion prompts of two kinds:

- "How would you use this?" connects the stage to the attendee's own classroom.
- "Why do you think this works?" has attendees reason about the motivation instead of only hearing it.

**Timing:** attendees arrive data-aware, so Notice is brief and the time goes to Investigate and Respond.

| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | Opening: are you data-driven? Define it. |
| 0:02–0:06 | Notice: Look, Ask |
| 0:06–0:12 | Investigate: Slice and download, Combine, Hypothesize |
| 0:12–0:18 | Respond: Plan, Test, Adjust |
| 0:18–0:20 | Close: resources and reflection |
