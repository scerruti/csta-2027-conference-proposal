# AP Score Reports as Instructional Data

## Session Type

Breakout Session (60 minutes)

## Abstract

AP Computer Science score reports provide valuable benchmarks, yet many teachers stop at reviewing averages, pass rates, and broad content categories. This proposal introduces a practical diagnostic workflow that helps AP Computer Science teachers move from score reports to instructional decisions.

Participants will learn how critical preparation decisions made before exporting College Board reports, such as separating students into score-performance bands, can reveal trends hidden by aggregate data. Using examples from AP Computer Science A (CSA) and AP Computer Science Principles (CSP), the session demonstrates how benchmark comparisons can identify meaningful data gaps and how prerequisite concept dependencies can help uncover the underlying instructional gaps causing those results.

Attendees will also be introduced to a free, privacy-focused browser application developed by a CSTA San Diego board member for a chapter workshop on AP score-report analysis. The tool aggregates AP Instructional Planning Reports locally, without uploading student information, and pairs with a companion planning worksheet that guides teachers from analysis to intervention planning. While informed by principles of the Data Wise Improvement Process, the focus remains on a practical AP Computer Science workflow that can be immediately applied to classroom planning and AP Classroom implementation.

This session aligns with CSTA Teacher Standards 4g (Instructional Design) and 5f (Classroom Practice) by using assessment data to identify learning gaps, analyze prerequisite concepts, and design responsive instructional interventions. It also supports equitable CS instruction by helping teachers examine score-performance patterns and provide targeted support to students who may be overlooked in aggregate reporting.

## Learning Goals

Participants will:

- Understand how score-band exports improve AP score-report analysis.
- Use benchmark comparisons to identify meaningful data patterns.
- Apply prerequisite concept analysis to diagnose instructional root causes.
- Access free tools and planning resources that support implementation.
- Translate AP score findings into action plans for classroom instruction and AP Classroom support.

## Educational Basis

This session is grounded in three steps of the Data Wise Improvement Process (Harvard Graduate School of Education):

- Step 2, Build Assessment Literacy: participants learn to read score bands, benchmarks, and the effects of multiple test forms before drawing conclusions.
- Step 4, Dig into Student Data: participants analyze score-band and benchmark data to locate specific patterns and misconceptions.
- Step 6, Develop Action Plan: participants choose targeted interventions connected to AP Classroom resources.

## Session Outline

### 0:00-0:08 | The AP Data Challenge

- Ask participants what they look for first when AP results arrive.
- Highlight the limitations of pass rates and aggregate averages.
- Introduce the challenge of moving from data to instructional action.
- Participant engagement: quick audience poll and discussion.

### 0:08-0:15 | Prepare Better Data Before Analysis

- Demonstrate score-band exports.
- Discuss why mixed-performance groups can hide instructional needs.
- Explain the impact of multiple test forms.
- Participant engagement: predict whether class-level or score-band analysis reveals more useful instructional information.

### 0:15-0:25 | Finding Data Gaps

- Demonstrate analysis using the AP Score Reports tool.
- Compare results against benchmark performance.
- Identify apparent areas for intervention.
- Participant engagement: participants identify which area they would prioritize first.

### 0:25-0:40 | The Diagnostic Challenge

- Present a CSA or CSP example showing multiple weak areas.
- Introduce the Content & Skills Matrix.
- Demonstrate how prerequisite dependencies can reveal root causes.
- Participant engagement: participants diagnose the likely instructional gap before it is revealed.

### 0:40-0:53 | Turning Insights into Action

- Introduce the Data Deep Dive worksheet.
- Walk through one completed example.
- Connect findings to AP Classroom interventions.
- Participant engagement: participants suggest a possible intervention strategy.

### 0:53-1:00 | Resources, Discussion, and Q&A

- Data Deep Dive worksheet: CSA and CSP Content & Skills Matrices, concept dependency checks, and action plan
- AP Score Reports browser tool (free, runs locally)
- Score-band export how-to
- Next steps and discussion

## Discussion Questions

- What patterns are hidden when we rely only on averages and pass rates?
- How can score-band analysis improve instructional planning?
- What do benchmark comparisons tell us that broad summary metrics do not?
- How can prerequisite concept analysis help us identify the root cause of student difficulty?
- What classroom moves can result from a stronger score-report workflow?

## Participant Outcomes

Participants will leave with:

- A clearer understanding of how to analyze AP score reports beyond averages.
- A process for identifying meaningful data gaps.
- A practical method for diagnosing instructional gaps using prerequisite patterns.
- Access to free tools and planning resources that can be immediately used in their AP classes.
