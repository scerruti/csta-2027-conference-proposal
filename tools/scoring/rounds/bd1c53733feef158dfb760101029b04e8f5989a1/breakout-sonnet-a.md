Count under the counting rules:
1. Data use as a cycle (Marsh, 2012). It is named, cited, and given a rationale.
2. Structured collaboration across schools with norms (Marsh, 2012). It is a distinct practice, with a rationale and a citation.
3. Formative assessment (Black & Wiliam, 1998). It is named, cited, and given a rationale.

The Data Wise ideas are not counted as a separate practice. Count: 4, which is at least 3.

Why not the next tier up: Top tier.

## 4. Engaging and Relevance

## 5. Takeaways
Quotes:
- "Tangible: slicing handout, browser tool, CSA and CSP worksheets, AP Classroom assessment handout. Intangible: confidence to repeat the cycle each year."
- "Slicing and download handout:** how to read the Subject Score Roster, turn what you see into a question (with sample questions and examples of questions the report can't answer)"
- "Participants use each resource during the session and take all four home to repeat the cycle each year."

Why not the next tier up: Top tier. Tangible and intangible takeaways are both named, and each of the four resources is described in detail. The one soft spot is that the intangible outcomes are stated briefly.

## 6. Attendee Preparedness
Quotes:
- "Participants leave with a hypothesis drawn from their AP results, an action plan, and a scheduled AP Classroom formative assessment."
- "If you can, bring a laptop and your College Board educator login; participants without access work alongside a partner."
- "Adjust happens after today: when results come back, reteach or spiral, and carry the results into future planning."

Why not the next tier up: Participants who attend with data should feel prepared. Those without access, or with portal problems in the 8-minute slicing step, may leave with less. The compressed hour and the reliance on live portal access make "extremely prepared" for all attendees hard to claim.

## 7. Overall Recommendation
Quotes:
- "Being data-driven means the data identifies where to look, the teacher's experience forms a hypothesis, and new data tests it."
- "Marsh found that educators most often lack support for moving from knowledge to action, so the session's second half is devoted to planning and building an assessment."
- "Results are expected to arrive around conference time, so the work starts with current data."

Why not the next tier up: Top tier. The proposal is well aligned to the standards, has a coherent hands-on design, and uses well-grounded practices and concrete resources. The remaining risks are tight pacing and dependence on portal access.
