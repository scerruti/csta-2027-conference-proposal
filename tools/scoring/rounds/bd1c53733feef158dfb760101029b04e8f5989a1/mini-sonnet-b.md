## 1. CSTA Standards
Quotes:
- "CSTA 4g, Inform instruction through assessment: Notice and Investigate (0:02–0:12) show how AP results inform instructional decisions."
- "CSTA 4f, Plan instruction to foster student understanding: Investigate and Respond (0:06–0:18) locate the likely gap in understanding, prioritize foundational concepts, and plan the instructional change."
- "CSTA 3f, Participate in CS professional learning communities: the Think-Pair-Share in each stage (0:02–0:18) has attendees share practice with peers"

Why not the next tier up: Three Teacher Standards are named by indicator and tied to timed segments, and 4g and 4f are strongly relevant. The 3f tie is a stretch: peer Think-Pair-Share is only loosely a professional learning community, and the 0:18–0:20 close is the only real PLC content. No Student Subtopics are given. That leaves "mostly relevant, with minor areas for improvement" rather than "thoroughly addressed."

## 3. Educational Best Practices / Pedagogy
Quotes:
- "**Data use as a cycle** (Marsh, 2012)."
- "**Formative assessment** (Black & Wiliam, 1998)."
- "**Think-Pair-Share** (Lyman, 1981)."

Count under the counting rules:
1. Data use as a cycle (Marsh, 2012): named, cited, and tied to the session's three stages. Counts.
2. Formative assessment (Black & Wiliam, 1998): named, cited, with a rationale about using evidence during the year. Counts.
3. Prioritizing foundational concepts (Cabo, 2015): named, cited, with a rationale. The study is a college Java course, but the proposal flags the transfer to AP CSA. Counts.
4. Think-Pair-Share (Lyman, 1981): named, cited, with a rationale. Counts.
- The Data Wise ideas are one framework, so they add no further count. Total: 4 distinct grounded practices, which is at least three, so tier 5.

Why not the next tier up: Top tier.

## 4. Engaging and Relevance
Quotes:

- "Think about the results you just received, or last year's. What question would you want them to answer?"
- "Your new results are arriving now. What is the first question you will ask of them?"

Why not the next tier up: It is engaging and timely for AP CSA and CSP teachers, and attendees work on their own results. The topic is narrow (AP score reports only), and the activities are mostly repeated Think-Pair-Share rather than something distinctly creative or innovative. That fits "engaging, relevant" better than "highly creative/innovative."

## 5. Takeaways

- "**Slicing and download handout:** how to read the Subject Score Roster, turn what you see into a question (with sample questions and examples of questions the report can't answer), filter the Instructional Planning Report by score band and section to match the question, and download it."
- "**Data Deep Dive worksheets, CSA and CSP versions:** each course's matrix of units and practices with sample skills in every cell, guidance on preferring earlier units, prerequisite checks, and an action-plan template."

Why not the next tier up: Top tier. The tangible and intangible takeaways are both stated, and all four resources are described in specific detail. The intangible side is brief but still clear.

## 6. Attendee Preparedness
Quotes:
- "Apply the first steps to their own context by drafting a question for their AP results and naming the slice that would answer it."
- "Plan: write an action plan for the hypothesis, fitted to your adopted curriculum."
- "Attendees leave with a slicing and download handout, the tool, CSA and CSP worksheets, and a guide to AP Classroom formative assessments."

Why not the next tier up: Attendees get a process, worksheets, and in-session practice, so they should feel prepared. In 20 minutes they only draft a question and name a slice, and the full investigate and respond steps are left for later. "Extremely prepared" is not supported.

## 7. Overall Recommendation
Quotes:
- "Knowing where students struggled on last year's AP exam is a start. Being data-driven means letting that knowledge change what you teach."
- "Marsh found that educators most often lack support for moving from knowledge to action"
- "An assessment is formative only when its results change what you teach."

Why not the next tier up: This is a well-grounded, practical, well-timed mini with strong resources and clear alignment. It is narrow in audience, the 3f alignment is thin, and it relies on a 20-minute overview of a multi-step process, so it falls short of top-5% distinction.
