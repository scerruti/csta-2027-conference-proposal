# CSTA 2027 Proposal Rubric Score Report

## Scope

This report records the scores of record from a blind review, following the [blind scoring procedure](Scoring-Instructions.md#blind-scoring-required-before-each-report). Each reviewer had only the [rubric tiers](Rubric-Tiers.md), one proposal's Markdown file, and that proposal's Presentation Outline PDF.

- [AP Score Reports as Instructional Data](../../projects/ap-score-reports/proposals/ap-score-reports-as-instructional-data/AP-Score-Reports-as-Instructional-Data.md): Session (1 hour), the guided, partner-based application of the process.
- [AP Scores to Instructional Action: A Quick Workflow](../../projects/ap-score-reports/proposals/ap-score-reports-quick-workflow/AP-Score-Reports-Quick-Workflow.md): Mini-Session (20 minutes), the reasoning behind the process.

Reviewer: a fresh subagent, model opus, one per proposal, run in parallel. This is round three. It ran after the standards were mapped to 4f, 4g, and 5f, the 2d link was removed, the 5f wording was changed to the AP Classroom question bank, and the breakout's support role, regroup, and take-home template were added.

## Compliance

Both proposals pass the submission check (`tools/validation/check_submission.py`) on every automated item. Manual items remain: the website form fields and the outline PDF upload.

## Review: AP Score Reports as Instructional Data

| Criterion | Score | Evidence and gaps |
|---|---:|---|
| CSTA Standards | 4/5 | 4g and 4f tied to Steps 2, 4, and 6. 5f tied to Step 7. No student subtopics. The reviewer found the 5f link a stretch, because the activity is teachers building an assessment, not students using feedback. |
| Content | 4/5 | Template used, with goals, discussion questions, and timed activities. Step 4 (0:11–0:31) is tight: login, data slice, export, import, cell circling, and dependency checks. The 0:31 regroup may split groups from their data-holder. |
| Educational Best Practices / Pedagogy | 3/5 | Data Wise is the only practice named and cited. Its four steps are parts of one framework. Collaborative inquiry and formative assessment appear in the activities but are not named or grounded. |
| Engaging and Relevance | 4/5 | Relevant and engaging for AP CS teachers. Participants without data become supporters and get a weaker experience. The grade level is implied by "AP," not stated. |
| Takeaways | 4/5 | Tangible resources are named. Only the worksheet's glossary is described in detail. The export guide and template are not. One intangible outcome. |
| Attendee Preparedness | 4/5 | Data-holders practise the full cycle on real data. Supporters watch and take home a template, so they may feel less ready. |
| Overall Recommendation | 4/5 | Recommend (top 25%). Step 4 timing, the 5f link, and one grounded practice keep it out of the top 5%. |

### Final Score: 27/35

### Improvements

- Reduce the Step 4 block, or move one of its activities out of the 20 minutes
- Confirm the regroup keeps each group's evidence, for example by having the data-holder carry it into the regrouped team
- Name and ground at least one more practice, if a source can be supplied
- Describe the export guide and action-plan template in the resources
- State the grade level

- Reconsider the 5f link, as for the breakout

## Note on Pedagogy Counting

The breakout was scored 3 and the mini 4 for the same four Data Wise steps. The mini reviewer's rationale says every practice comes from one framework, which points to 3. The blind score stands as recorded, and the inconsistency is flagged for the next round. The counting rule should be fixed before that round, and the same reviewer should score both proposals.
